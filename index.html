About Be™ Brooklyn Equine

Nestled in the heart of Prospect Park, we're Brooklyn's only centrally located stable, bringing equestrian experiences right to your doorstep. Whether you're looking to try riding for the first time or continue your journey as an experienced rider, our team is here to guide you.

Our mission is to make horse care and riding programs accessible, fun, and safe for riders of all ages and abilities. We believe that horses have a way of transforming lives by building confidence, patience, and connection with nature.
